Privacy

What we do withyour details.

A name and a number, so we can call you back or tell you about a property. Nothing sold, nothing handed to anyone who does not need it.

The short version. Analytics only runs if you accept cookies. This site has two forms: one to book a consultation, and one for buyers who want to hear about properties. Both arrive as an email in our inbox, and we keep a working copy in our own records so we can follow up. We use Google Analytics to count visits. We have never sold or rented anyone's details and we are not going to start.

Want yours removed? Email mojomarketingco@gmail.com and we will do it.

Who we are

Mojo Marketing is a property marketing company operating across Israel. We are not a real estate agency and we do not take commission. This policy covers themojomarketing.com, the enquiries that come through it, and the people we speak to while marketing a property.

For anything to do with your personal details — a question, a correction, a deletion — email mojomarketingco@gmail.com or message Mordechai on 050-687-5631. One of the two of us reads it. There is no department to be routed through.

What our forms ask for

Book a consultation (for owners selling or renting out a property):

  • Your full name and a phone number. Both required — we cannot call you back without them.
  • An email address, optional.
  • Anything you type in the message box. Most people put the property's location and whether they are selling or renting.

Looking for a property (on the Featured Properties page):

  • Your full name and a phone number, required.
  • Optionally: an email address, whether you want to buy or rent, the area, your budget, the number of rooms, and anything else you want us to know.
  • Which of our properties you are asking about, if any.

That is the whole list. We do not ask for ID numbers, bank details or proof of funds, and you should not send them to us.

Where those details actually go

When you submit a form it does not travel straight to us. It passes through Web3Forms, a form service that receives the submission and emails it on to our business inbox. So Web3Forms handles your details too, and holds submissions on its own systems for up to three years under its own policy. We chose it because it does not add advertising trackers to the page.

From there the email sits in our Gmail account, which is Google. We also copy the details into our own records — a spreadsheet and an internal tool, both held in Google's services and seen only by the two of us — so we can follow up, keep track of who asked about what, and match buyers to properties. If you reply to us, or message us on WhatsApp, that conversation sits with Google or with Meta in the usual way. These companies operate internationally, so your details may be handled outside Israel.

If you are looking for a property

When you fill in the buyer form, we add you to our buyers list. We use it for one thing: when a property we are marketing matches what you told us, we call or message you about it. We do not send newsletters, and we do not hand the list to anyone.

If you ask about a specific property, we pass your name and what you told us to that property's owner, so they know who is interested. That is the only person it goes to.

We also look for buyers ourselves. If you post publicly — in a community group, on social media or on a listing site — that you are looking for a property, and one of ours matches, we may contact you about it and note your post and contact details in our records. If you would rather we did not, tell us once and we will remove you and not contact you again.

If you become a client

To market a property we need its details and photographs, and we publish those — to Yad2, Madlan, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, our own buyers list, and on a sign at the property. That is the job, and you decide what goes in.

What we do not publish is you. Your name and phone number do not go into the listings or on the sign; enquiries come to us instead of to you. We pass on the serious ones with our notes, you can see every enquiry if you want to, and we send you a report every week.

How long we keep them

Emails do not delete themselves, and neither do our records. Unless you ask us to remove them, enquiries stay in our inbox and our records while they might still be useful — for a buyer, while you are still looking; for an owner, while we are working together and for a while after. We do not keep working files on a property once its campaign has finished.

Ask us and it goes, from the inbox and from our records. See your rights below.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they read, and roughly where in the world they are — at the level of a country or a city, worked out from your IP address. It tells us that forty people read the pricing page this week. It does not tell us who they were.

Google Analytics only runs if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose No thanks, it is not loaded at all, no analytics cookies are set, and nothing about your visit is sent to Google. The site works exactly the same either way. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

Your choice is remembered in your own browser so we do not ask again. Google Analytics cookies are the only cookies on the site. We run no advertising pixels, no remarketing tags, and nothing from Meta.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may log the IP addresses of visitors for security and abuse prevention. We do not have access to those logs.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or trade your details. Not to agents, not to mortgage brokers, not to anyone.
  • Add you to a newsletter or mailing list. If we ever start one, you will have to ask for it.
  • Pass your details to an agent or any other third party. The one exception is the owner of a property you asked us about, as described above.

Your rights, and how to use them

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law — and, if you are in the UK or the EU, under the GDPR — you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. You do not need a reason and you do not need to explain yourself.

Email mojomarketingco@gmail.com with what you want. We will come back to you within thirty days, and in practice much sooner. If you ask for deletion we will delete you from our inbox and our records, take you off the buyers list, and tell you it is done.

Changes to this policy

If what we do changes — a new tool, a different form provider, advertising, anything that moves your details somewhere new — we will update this page and change the date below. We will not quietly broaden it.
