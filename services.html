What we do

Eleven things, all included,for one flat fee.

No tiers. Every campaign gets all of it, for up to six months, with one person running it and a report every week. No percentage. Ever.

We prepare it

01

A full CMA, before anything else

We find what your property is really worth: what sold nearby, what is sitting unsold and why. Price it wrong and nothing after that can fix it.

02

Professional photography

Buyers decide in about three seconds, on a phone. That decision is made almost entirely on the first photo.

03

A walkthrough video

Where we started, and still where we are strongest. Buyers who show up have already decided they like it.

We find your buyer

04

Every channel, on paid packages

The main property sites and social media. We pay for premium placement on the property sites, so your listing sits above the free private ones without you paying for it.

05

Our exclusive network

An exclusive network of community WhatsApp groups and contacts across the neighborhoods we work in, where a lot of the demand moves before it ever reaches a listing site.

06

A sign at the property

A large, weatherproof sign on the building. Plenty of buyers start by walking the streets they want to live on.

07

We go looking for buyers

We do not only wait for calls. We scan social media, listing sites and the groups for people looking for what you are selling, check them against our own buyers list, and call them.

We see it through

08

Every enquiry handled, your position protected

Enquiries come to us, and you still see all of them. Sellers lose money on the first phone call, not at the table, so we know which questions to answer and which to leave alone.

09

Negotiation

When offers arrive, we negotiate with you: what to hold, what to give, and when, and we can handle the back-and-forth with the buyer. The final decision is always yours, and the contract is drawn up by your lawyer, as with any sale.

10

Showings, if you want them

Run your own, or hand them to us. Included either way.

11

One person, start to finish

One named person from the first call to the sale, with a direct number, and a weekly report on reach, enquiries, offers and what we would do next. For up to six months, you watch us work.
