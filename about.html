About us

One person on your property,from the first call to the sale.

Not an agency. Not a listing service. A property marketing company that charges a flat fee instead of a percentage, and gives every owner someone who knows their property as well as they do.

We started out shooting property videos and running CMAs for agents.

Doing that, we saw how much of a sale is decided by the marketing: the first photo, the first call, the buyer who never would have seen it otherwise. And we saw that owners wanted that part done properly, by someone who stayed with them, without paying a percentage of their home for it.

So that is what we built. Every property gets one named person, from the first conversation to the sale. They plan the campaign, field every enquiry, go looking for buyers, help you think through every offer, and send you a report every week. You always know who to call, and they always know your property.

We specialize in Anglo neighborhoods throughout Israel, for both sales and rentals, and we work in Hebrew and English.

What we are not

Not a real estate agency. Not a listing service. We never take commission, and we do not ask for an exclusive agreement.

The team

The peoplebehind every campaign.

Mordechai leads the shoot and the creative. Yosef leads strategy and the client relationship. Whichever of us is your named person stays with you from start to finish. No account manager layer, and no being passed around.

Hebrew and English are both native here, which is why the marketing runs properly in both rather than being translated into one of them.

Every week on the market at the wrong price costs more than this entire campaign.
