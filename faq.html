FAQ

The thingspeople actually ask.

Including the ones people are too polite to ask on a call.

Why would I not just use an agent?

An agent handles showings, negotiation, and paperwork, and takes a percentage of your sale for all of it, marketing included. We handle the marketing, properly, for a flat fee. Many of our clients sell without an agent. Some use us alongside one and get far better marketing than the agent would have produced.

Are you a real estate agency?

No. We are a property marketing company. Not an agency, not a listing service. We never take commission.

Is the success fee just a commission?

No. A commission is a percentage of your sale price, so it rises with the value of your property and you do not know the figure until you sell. The success fee is fixed — 2,500 ₪ on a sale, 1,250 ₪ on a rental — regardless of what the property goes for, and it is only payable if it goes at all.

What if it does not sell?

You pay the campaign fee and nothing else. The success fee is only charged on a completed sale or rental.

Can I extend my campaign past 60 days?

Yes. For a sale, one extra month is 1,500 ₪, and every month after that is the same rate. Two months booked together is 2,500 ₪, which saves you 500 ₪.

For a rental, every extra month is 750 ₪.

Worth saying plainly: if a rental has not gone in sixty days, the problem is usually the asking rent rather than the marketing. We will tell you that rather than sell you an extension.

How long does a campaign run?

Campaigns run for 60 days.

Do I have to sign an exclusive agreement?

No. You are never locked into an exclusive agreement with Mojo.

What does handling leads actually mean?

Enquiries come to us instead of to you. We speak to everyone who calls, filter out the people who are not serious, and pass on only the buyers worth your time.

It also protects your position. Sellers taking their own calls tend to give things away without realising — how long it has been listed, when they need to move, whether they would take less. We know which questions to answer and which to leave alone. We are not negotiating for you; we are making sure you start the negotiation with everything still in your hand.

Do you run the showings?

We can. Showings are included in both packages, and it is your call — run them yourself, or hand them to us.

Where do you advertise?

Local WhatsApp groups in your area, Yad2, Madlan, Facebook, Instagram, paid social campaigns, and direct distribution into curated buyer and investor networks.

How do you keep me updated?

Weekly reporting. You get clear updates on reach, interest, and next steps every week, plus every enquiry forwarded to you as it comes in.

How fast can you launch?

Within 48 hours of your property visit and content creation.

I live abroad. Can you still work with me?

Yes, and a large part of our client base is in exactly that position. We handle the photography, the listings, and the campaign on the ground, and you get weekly updates wherever you are.

Which areas do you serve?

All over Israel. Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Beit Shemesh, Modiin, Netanya, Ra'anana, Haifa, Ashdod, Herzliya, and everywhere in between.

What property types do you work with?

Apartments, houses, and commercial properties across all price points, for both sale and rental.

Can I buy services individually?

Yes. Individual services are available on request, priced per project.

I have more than one property.

We do package pricing for multiple properties. Get in touch and we will put something together.
